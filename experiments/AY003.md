# AY003: Sensor Stabilisation and Baseline Characterisation Experiment

## Objective

To determine the warm-up period of the BME688 gas sensor and identify when the baseline gas resistance reaches a stable plateau suitable for breath measurements.

## Research Question

**How long does the BME688 sensor require after activation before measurements become stable?**

## Experimental Setup

### Hardware

* ESP32 microcontroller
* BME688 gas sensor
* TFT display

### Software

* Arduino firmware
* Python data logging pipeline
* CSV data storage
* Matplotlib visualisation

The sensor was operated under normal environmental conditions without breath exposure to observe natural baseline behaviour.

## Method

1. The BME688 sensor was powered on.
2. Baseline gas resistance measurements were continuously recorded for 45 minutes.
3. Sensor output was logged over time.
4. Gas resistance values were plotted against time.
5. The period of initial drift and subsequent stabilisation were analysed.

## Observations

### Initial Baseline Drift

Following activation, the sensor exhibited changes in gas resistance over time.

This initial variation represents the sensor warm-up period and environmental equilibration.

### Stabilisation Phase

After the initial drift period, the gas resistance trend began to plateau, indicating a more stable operating region.

The stabilisation point will be determined by analysing when changes in baseline measurements become minimal.

## Results Summary

The experiment aims to determine:

* Sensor warm-up time
* Baseline drift magnitude
* Stable measurement window for breath experiments

## Impact on OHX Development

Understanding sensor stabilisation is essential for reliable breath data collection.

The results will define the standard operating conditions for future experiments by ensuring that breath measurements are collected after the sensor has reached a stable baseline.

## Next Steps

* Repeat baseline stabilisation experiments to assess consistency
* Calculate baseline variation after stabilisation
* Define sensor warm-up time for the OHX breath collection protocol
* Begin structured breath dataset collection
